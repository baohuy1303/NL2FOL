- Scrip doen't learn from CSVs. All prompting, the CSVs are imput examples.
Ex:
python src/nl_to_fol.py 
--model_name gpt-4o 
--nli_model_name roberta-large-mnli 
--run_name demo 
--dataset logic 
--length 10

Reads 10 “fallacy/negative” texts from data/fallacies.csv
Reads 10 “positive entailment” texts from data/nli_entailments_test.csv
Merges and normalizes text into articles. Saves this combined sample as 
dataset.csv

Each row's article column is ran through GPT to extract claim and implication. 
Then NLI classifies relations between claim and implication.
Logical Form 1 (LF1) is tighter and precise.
Logical Form 2 (LF2) is more robust but less specific. (when mappings are uncertain)
